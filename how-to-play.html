How to play

Bolo Mat! takes one minute to learn.

The steps

  1. One person creates a room and shares the code or WhatsApp link.
  2. Everyone joins on their own phone and picks a team.
  3. Each turn, one player is the describer and gets 5 words.
  4. They describe the words without saying them. Teammates type guesses.
  5. First team to 40 points wins.

Scoring

Correct word+1
Perfect spelling+1 more
Max per turn10
Small typosstill count

Tips

  • Skip a hard word and move on fast.
  • Use examples: “you eat it with chutney”.
  • Rivals must stay quiet!
  • Host can pick desi-only or global-only words.

FAQ

Do I need an app?

No. It runs in your browser on any phone or laptop.

Why can’t I join a room?

The game may have already started, or the code is wrong. Ask the host for a fresh link.

We are only 2 or 3 people. Can we play?

Yes. Pick Duel for 2 players: you take turns describing and the other person guesses. Pick Co-op for 2 or more: everyone is one squad, and you try to beat the goal together. Teams mode needs 4 or more players.

Can I play alone?

Try the Daily Clue, a solo word puzzle that changes every day.

What if the host leaves?

Another player becomes host automatically.
